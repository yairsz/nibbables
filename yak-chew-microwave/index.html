Yak chew microwave instructions: what each brand says, quoted

Several yak cheese chew brands tell owners to puff the last small piece of a chew in the microwave. Their instructions don't all match: times run from 30 to 90 seconds, and some say to soak the piece first while others don't mention it. Below, each source's own wording is quoted side by side and linked. Nibbables hasn't tested any of these methods; follow the instructions for the brand you bought and ask your vet if you're unsure.

Last checked 2026-10-23 · page updated 2026-10-24

Before you give any chew: supervise your dog while it chews, choose a size and hardness that suits your dog (follow the brand's size guidance), take away small or broken pieces, keep fresh water available, and ask your vet if a chew is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental work or dogs on special diets. How long do you microwave a yak chew? Here's what the brands' own instructions say.

First, the instructions we found all describe the leftover end piece, not a whole chew.

Times run from 30 to 90 seconds. Himalayan Dog Chew and Pawstruck say 45 seconds. Mighty Paw says 45 to 60. Yak Chewz says 60, and not more than 90, due to burning.

Soak it first? Himalayan Dog Chew, Yak Chewz, Mighty Paw and Yakkity Zac's say about five minutes. Pawstruck and Yakers don't mention it.

Every source says to let it cool before your dog gets it. Mighty Paw and Himalayan Pet Supply also say to supervise.

All seven sources, quoted word for word, are at nibbables dot com. Follow the brand you bought, and ask your vet if you're unsure.

Can you microwave a Himalayan dog chew?

The brand behind the Himalayan Dog Chew name gives microwave instructions for the leftover end piece, not the whole chew. Himalayan Pet Supply's Cheese product page says “After your dog enjoys their chew, take the leftover piece away”, then “Soak in warm water for 5 minutes” and “Heat for 45 seconds in a 1000-watt microwave”. Pawstruck, which sells a “Medium Himalayan Yak Dog Chew”, says “Put the piece in the microwave for 45 seconds”. The other five sources quoted on this page also give microwave times for yak cheese chews, from 30 to 90 seconds, and none of them describes microwaving a whole chew. Nibbables hasn't tested any of these methods; follow the instructions for the chew you bought and ask your vet if you're unsure.

How long to microwave a yak chew?

It depends on whose instructions you follow. In the seven sources above, times run from 30 to 90 seconds: Himalayan Dog Chew and Pawstruck each say 45 seconds (Himalayan Pet Supply specifies a 1000-watt microwave), Mighty Paw says 45-60 seconds, YAKERS 30-60 seconds, Yak Chewz 60 seconds plus an optional 30 more, Yakkity Zac's approximately 45–90 seconds, and Whole Dog Journal roughly 30 to 90 seconds depending on the piece's size.

How long to soak a yak chew before microwaving?

Himalayan Dog Chew, Yak Chewz, Mighty Paw and Yakkity Zac's each say about 5 minutes (Himalayan Pet Supply specifies warm water). Whole Dog Journal says 5 to 10 minutes in warm water. The Pawstruck and YAKERS instructions quoted here don't mention soaking.

Is there a maximum microwave time for a yak chew?

Of the sources here, only Yak Chewz gives an upper limit: “Do not exceed 90 seconds due to burning”. Mighty Paw's guide says “Observe the puff while microwaving, and adjust your time as needed to prevent overheating.” Yakkity Zac's says “Watch it as it cooks.” and “Microwave times vary depending on the power of your microwave and the size of the piece.”

Can you microwave a whole yak chew?

None of these sources describes microwaving a whole chew; they all describe the leftover end piece. Yakkity Zac's answers the question directly: “The full-sized chew is designed to be enjoyed in its original hard form.” YAKERS describes the piece as “approximately 3-4cm of a medium or XL chew”, and Pawstruck puts its microwave instructions under the heading “Precaution to Avoid Choking”.

Why does a yak chew puff up in the microwave?

Yakkity Zac's explains it this way: “When you soak the final piece and then heat it in the microwave, the remaining moisture inside rapidly turns to steam.” Mighty Paw says the piece “can puff up to three times its size”.

Cooling and supervision

Every source quoted above says to let the puff cool before your dog gets it. Yakkity Zac's adds: “Never give the puffed chew to your dog while it's still hot.” Mighty Paw's guide says: “Always supervise your dog when giving them treats, especially homemade ones.” Himalayan Pet Supply's advice for use includes “Supervise pets when giving them any treat”.

Mighty Paw's yak cheese product page gives a shorter version of its method: “Soak it in water for 5 minutes, then microwave for 45–60 seconds to create a crunchy cheese puff your dog will love.”

Yak chew pages that don't mention microwaving

The product pages for the Pupford, Raw Paws Pet Food and Best Bully Sticks yak chews in our directory didn't mention microwaving when we checked them on 2026-10-23. If you have one of those, check with the brand before puffing it. You can compare all the yak chews we list, with ingredients and origin claims, on our yak cheese chews page.
