Nibbables privacy policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Nibbables (nibbables.com) is published by 2 Handed Coding, LLC. This page explains what information is collected when you visit, by whom, and why. Questions or requests: developer@2handedcoding.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, sign-ups, comment boxes, newsletters, contact forms or payments on Nibbables. We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and Microsoft Clarity to see how pages are used (clicks and scrolling). They use cookies.
  • Videos are click-to-play. Nothing from the YouTube player loads until you press play.
  • Our host, GitHub Pages, logs visitors' IP addresses for its own security purposes.
  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't show ads from ad networks.

Google Analytics

Every page loads Google Analytics 4 (Google tag gtag.js, measurement ID G-MXKFFSYRQB) from www.googletagmanager.com. We use it only to count visits and see which pages are useful. It's provided by Google LLC.

  • What it collects: the pages you view and the standard events Google Analytics records automatically (for example page views, scrolls and outbound link clicks), the page that referred you, your browser and device type, screen size, language, and an approximate location (country, region or city) that Google derives from your IP address. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
  • Cookies: Google Analytics sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_<ID> on nibbables.com to tell one visit from another. Google documents a lifetime of up to 2 years for these cookies.
  • Our settings: Google signals is turned off, event-level data is kept for 2 months (the shortest option Google Analytics offers), and the property isn't linked to Google Ads. The account's data-sharing settings are on, which lets Google use the data as described in Google Analytics data-sharing settings.
  • Clicks to other sites: when you click a link that leaves nibbables.com, the page sends Google Analytics an event so we can tell whether anyone uses the links we list. For affiliate links the event records the destination's domain name and which product card the link sat in; for other links it records the destination's domain name and full URL. It sends no name, email address or other identifier, and it isn't tied to a profile of you.
  • What we see: aggregate reports (page views, traffic sources, countries, device types). We don't see your name, email address or IP address.
  • Opting out: you can block or delete cookies in your browser, use a content blocker, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google explains its practices in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Microsoft Clarity

Every page also loads Microsoft Clarity (project ID z7gtb12n7l) from www.clarity.ms. It's provided by Microsoft. We use it to see how visitors use the site, for example which parts of a page people click or scroll to, and which traffic channels (including links from AI chat tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity) send visitors here.

  • What it collects: Microsoft says Clarity "captures the user interactions on your website such as, how the page is rendering, user interactions such as mouse movements, clicks, scrolls". Clarity uses this to build session recordings and heatmaps. Text typed into input boxes is masked. Microsoft says recordings are kept for 30 days (a sample for up to 9 months) and heatmaps for up to 9 months.
  • Cookies: Clarity sets first-party cookies named _clck (keeps a Clarity user ID for this site) and _clsk (links page views into one session). Microsoft also lists third-party cookies (CLID, ANONCHK, MR, MUID, SM) that Clarity may set; Microsoft says MUID is used "for advertising, site analytics, and other operational purposes". If you've disabled cookies in your browser, Clarity doesn't set them. See Microsoft's Clarity cookie list.
  • What we see: Clarity dashboards, heatmaps and session recordings. We don't use Clarity to collect your name or email address.
  • Opting out: Microsoft says you can opt out of Clarity through the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out system (choose Microsoft), and that Clarity supports Global Privacy Control (GPC). Microsoft also says Clarity doesn't currently respond to browser Do-Not-Track signals. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser or use a content blocker. Details are in the Clarity FAQ and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Amazon affiliate links

Nibbables is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

  • The "Check on Amazon" buttons on product cards are affiliate links to amazon.com that include our Associates tag (nibbables-20).
  • Nibbables doesn't load any Amazon scripts, images or widgets, and our pages don't set Amazon cookies. Nothing is sent to Amazon unless you click one of those links.
  • When you click one, you leave Nibbables for amazon.com. Amazon may set or read cookies on your device to record that you came from our link and to credit qualifying purchases, as described in the Amazon.com Privacy Notice (which links to Amazon's Cookies Notice).
  • Amazon gives us aggregate reports, such as the number of clicks and which items were ordered, so we can be paid. Those reports don't tell us who you are.

Hosting and server logs

Nibbables is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub (see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement). We don't receive or have access to those logs.

Things that stay in your browser

The compare page has a chew-type menu, checkboxes and a search box for narrowing the table. It runs entirely in your browser: what you type or select isn't sent to us or stored. (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity may record that you interacted with the page, as described above, but not the text you type.)

If you email us

If you email us (for example to report an out-of-date listing), we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to fix the listing. We don't add it to a mailing list or share it.

Links to other websites

Nibbables links to many other websites: the organizations and brands we list, their sources, and other services. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy doesn't cover.

Children

Nibbables isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and requests

You can control cookies through your browser settings and the Google Analytics opt-out add-on linked above. If you have a question about this policy, or want to make a request about your information, email developer@2handedcoding.com. Keep in mind that we don't hold analytics data tied to your name or email, and we can't access GitHub's server logs, so for data held by Google, Microsoft or GitHub you may need to contact them directly.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting something new (for example a new analytics tool, embed or affiliate program), we'll update this page and the effective date above before or when the change goes live.
