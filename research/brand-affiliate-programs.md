# Nibbables: brand and retailer affiliate programs

Checked Fri 2026-10-23, about 2:30 to 3:15 PM ET. Method: each brand's own site (homepage, footer links, Shopify page sitemaps,
affiliate/terms pages) fetched with curl, plus network profile pages (Awin public merchant profiles, UpPromote form) and WebSearch
or WebFetch where sites block curl. No sign-ups, no contact with anyone.
Rules: terms are quoted only as the brand or network page states them. Third-party directory figures (Affilitizer, Lasso, FMTC, etc.)
are labelled **third-party** and are not confirmed terms. Anything not found is marked **unknown**.

Catalog: data/products.json, 34 products across 12 brands. Product counts: GREAT DOG CO. 5, Best Bully Sticks 5, Redbarn 4,
Earth Animal 4, Mighty Paw 3, Raw Paws 3, Campfire Treats 2, Pawstruck 2, Nature Gnaws 2, Pupford 2, Jack&Pup 1, Bow Wow Labs 1.
Already in place: Amazon Associates `nibbables-20` (25 of 34 linked, see amazon-matches.md), CJ (Raw Paws applied), Awin publisher signup in progress.

"Sign-up needs" column:
- **Network acct**: a network publisher account (Impact, Rakuten, Awin, FlexOffers) that collects Yair's tax and payout details. Yair has to do this himself.
- **Form**: the brand's own form (name, email, address, website). The brand page names PayPal for payouts, and the PayPal account is Yair's.
- **Email**: no form; you email the brand.
