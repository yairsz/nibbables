Yak (Himalayan) cheese chews: ingredients and origin by brand

Yak cheese chews are hard cheese chews that the brands describe as made from yak and/or cow milk, salt and lime juice. Ingredient lists differ: some brands list yak milk only, others yak and cow milk. Each card quotes the brand's own page.

8 products · every claim is quoted from the brand's own page and linked · no prices (they change; check the brand) · page updated 2026-10-27 · compare all in one table

Before you give any chew: supervise your dog while it chews, choose a size and hardness that suits your dog (follow the brand's size guidance), take away small or broken pieces, keep fresh water available, and ask your vet if a chew is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental work or dogs on special diets.
