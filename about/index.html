About Nibbables: how we verify, and our affiliate disclosure

How we verify

  • Every product comes from the brand's own website, never a reseller or review site. Each card links the exact product page we read and shows the date we checked it.
  • Claims are quoted word for word in quotation marks: ingredients, "made in" and "sourced from" wording, rawhide-free statements, size guidance and supervision notes. We don't reword them into our own claims.
  • If the page doesn't state something, the card says Not stated, check with them. We never guess or fill gaps from other sources.
  • "Rawhide-free" is shown only when the brand's page says so explicitly ("rawhide-free", "made without rawhide" and similar). Many other chews here may also contain no rawhide. Their pages just don't say it in those words.
  • "Made in USA", "Sourced and made in the USA", "Country of Origin" and similar statements are the brand's claims. We haven't independently verified where any product is made or sourced. Where a page contradicts itself, the card notes it.
  • We show no prices, because they change. We show no reviews, star ratings or safety ratings.
  • Our build checks every listing for a source and every required field, and refuses to publish a listing that is missing one.

Chewing and your dog

Before you give any chew: supervise your dog while it chews, choose a size and hardness that suits your dog (follow the brand's size guidance), take away small or broken pieces, keep fresh water available, and ask your vet if a chew is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental work or dogs on special diets. Nibbables does not rate safety. Each card shows what the brand itself says.

Nibbables is an information directory, not veterinary advice. No chew is right for every dog. Supervise chewing, choose an appropriate size, and talk to your vet, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental problems or dogs on special diets.
