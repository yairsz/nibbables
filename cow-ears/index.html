Cow ear dog chews: what each brand says about origin, ingredients and sizes

Nibbables editorial team. Every claim on this page is quoted from the brand's own page or the source linked next to it; we don't test, rate or review products. Page updated 2026-11-05. How we verify.

At a glanceAll products

Cow ears are dried or oven-baked beef ears, sold whole, plain or washed ("white"), and thick-cut. If you're asking whether cow ears are good for dogs, or how they compare with pig ears, we compare only what each brand's own page states: the ingredients, where the ears come from or are made, calorie and guaranteed-analysis figures, and the chewer sizes and supervision steps each brand names. Stated origins differ a lot between brands: Brazil, Colombia, Canada, the USA and Turkey all appear. Only one page gives calories per ear. Anything a page doesn't say is marked Not stated. Ask your vet whether a cow ear suits your dog.

7 products · every claim is quoted from the brand's own page and linked · no prices (they change; check the brand) · compare all in one table

Before you give any chew: supervise your dog while it chews, choose a size and hardness that suits your dog (follow the brand's size guidance), take away small or broken pieces, keep fresh water available, and ask your vet if a chew is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental work or dogs on special diets.
