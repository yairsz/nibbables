Does Nibbables recommend a chew for my dog's size?

No. Nibbables groups chews only by the words each brand uses on its own product page and links that page. It doesn't rank chews or rate their safety. Pick a size by the brand's guidance and ask your vet.

How we grouped these

We read the "Size / dog guidance" line we quote from each brand's product page and put the chew in every group whose words appear in it (for example "puppies", "small dogs", "power chewers", "senior" or a weight in pounds). We don't add groups the brand doesn't name, and being in a group is not a recommendation.
