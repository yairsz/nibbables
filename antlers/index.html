Elk and deer antler chews: sourcing claims by brand

Antler chews are made from elk or deer antler. Most brands here describe their antlers as naturally shed, and several describe them as durable chews for strong chewers. Check each brand's size guidance, and ask your vet whether a hard chew suits your dog's teeth.

7 products · every claim is quoted from the brand's own page and linked · no prices (they change; check the brand) · page updated 2026-10-23 · compare all in one table

Before you give any chew: supervise your dog while it chews, choose a size and hardness that suits your dog (follow the brand's size guidance), take away small or broken pieces, keep fresh water available, and ask your vet if a chew is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental work or dogs on special diets.
