Elk and deer antler chews: sourcing claims by brand

Antler chews are made from elk or deer antler. Most brands here describe their antlers as naturally shed, and several describe them as durable chews for strong chewers. Check each brand's size guidance, and ask your vet whether a hard chew suits your dog's teeth.
