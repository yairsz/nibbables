At a glanceAll products

Cow hooves are cleaned, dried or baked beef hooves, sold plain or with a filling in the hollow center. If you're asking whether cow hooves are good for dogs, we leave each brand's claims on its own page and compare only what the pages state: the ingredients, where the hooves come from and where they're made, the fat and calorie figures, and which chewers each brand names. Plain and filled hooves differ a lot: the plain hooves list one ingredient, while the filled ones list a filling with corn syrup, sugar and preservatives. Stated origins include Brazil, Australia and the USA. Pawstruck's page warns that natural hooves may splinter or break. Anything a page doesn't say is marked Not stated. Ask your vet whether a hoof suits your dog.
