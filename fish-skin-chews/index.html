Fish skin dog chews: what cod, wolffish and salmon skin brands say

Nibbables editorial team. Every claim on this page is quoted from the brand's own page or the source linked next to it; we don't test, rate or review products. Page updated 2026-11-05. How we verify.

At a glanceAll products

Fish skin chews are dried cod, wolffish or salmon skins, sold rolled, wrapped into sticks or as whole skins. If you're asking whether fish skins are good for dogs, the brand pages below make nutrition claims (omega-3s, protein, dental support) in their own words, and we leave those claims on their pages rather than repeat them as fact. What we compare is what each page states: the one ingredient, where the fish is from and where the chew is made, the fat and calorie figures, and which dog sizes it names. The fat figures differ a lot: the cod and wolffish skins here list 1% to 4.49% fat, while Jack's Premium lists 18% for its smoked salmon skins. Anything a page doesn't say is marked Not stated. Ask your vet whether a fish skin chew suits your dog.

Before you give any chew: supervise your dog while it chews, choose a size and hardness that suits your dog (follow the brand's size guidance), take away small or broken pieces, keep fresh water available, and ask your vet if a chew is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental work or dogs on special diets.
