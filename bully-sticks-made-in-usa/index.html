Bully sticks made in the USA: each brand's exact wording

“Made in USA” on a bully stick can mean different things: some brands say the stick is made and sourced in the USA, some say only where it was baked, and some say it was packaged here from imported beef. This page quotes the made-in and sourcing wording from 18 bully stick product pages, side by side, plus what the FTC says a Made in USA claim requires. These are the brands' claims, not our findings, and we don't rank them.

What a “Made in USA” claim is supposed to mean

The Federal Trade Commission's guidance says that for a product to be called Made in USA without qualification, “the product must be “all or virtually all” made in the U.S.” It defines that as meaning “the final assembly or processing of the product occurs in the United States, all significant processing that goes into the product occurs in the United States, and all or virtually all ingredients or components of the product are made and sourced in the United States.”

The FTC also says “A company doesn’t need approval from the FTC before making a Made in USA claim”, so a claim on a product page is the company's own statement. Its guidance gives a packaging example: “Stating Made in USA on the package would deceive consumers about the origin of the product inside.” We quote the FTC so you can read each brand's wording against it; we don't judge whether any brand meets the standard.

Made, sourced, baked or packaged: the wording differs

Seven of the 18 pages say both made and sourced in the USA. Two say only where the stick is made or baked: Pupford (“This product is made in the USA.”) and Best Bully Sticks' 12-inch stick (“USA-baked”), and our card for the Best Bully Sticks stick notes the page doesn't say where the beef comes from.

Two pages mention the USA in a narrower way. Bully Bunches says “Packaged in the USA; product of Brazil, Turkey or Colombia.” Bow Wow Labs lists “Sourced from Brazil, Argentina, the USA, & Turkey”, and its FAQ says the sticks “are packaged in the US”. Mighty Paw names Colombia (and, elsewhere on the page, Brazil) as its source and says the sticks are “Hand-inspected in our Rochester, NY headquarters”. None of these three says the stick is made in the USA.
