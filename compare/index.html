Before you give your dog a long-lasting chew, like a bully stick, yak chew or antler, run through this quick checklist.

One. Supervise your dog while it chews.

Two. Choose a size and hardness that suits your dog, and follow the brand's size guidance.

Three. Take away small or broken pieces. Four. Keep fresh water available.

Five. Ask your vet if a chew is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, dogs with dental work, or dogs on special diets.

Then check what the brand actually says: the ingredients, where it says the chew is made and sourced, and its size and supervision notes.
