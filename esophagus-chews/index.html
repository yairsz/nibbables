Gullet sticks and beef esophagus chews: origin and ingredients by brand

Gullet sticks are dog chews that brands describe as made from beef gullet or beef esophagus; some brands call it weasand, which Jack's Premium describes as "the muscle meat that surrounds the esophagus." One product here, Redbarn's Collagen Wrapped Esophagus Stick, is collagen from beef hide wrapped with esophagus. Origin claims, sizes and pack counts differ by brand.
